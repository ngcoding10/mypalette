Allergies, diets and health

Allergies and diets. Allergy and diet flags are a best guess from a photo or typed menu and general food knowledge. Kitchens and recipes vary, and a photo can be misread. MyPalette never tells you a dish is safe. Always confirm with the staff before you order. This is not medical advice.

Suggestions, descriptions and flags are generated automatically from a photo or typed text and general food knowledge. They can be wrong or incomplete: a photo can be misread, a dish can be made differently, and ingredients can be hidden in sauces. Do not rely on MyPalette for anything that affects your health.
